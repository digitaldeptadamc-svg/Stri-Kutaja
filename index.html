Department of Dravyaguna
Stri Kutaja
Botanical Name :

  • Wrightia tinctoria (Roxb.) R.Br.

    • Family Name :

  • Apocynaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Strī Kuṭaja

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Dudi

  • English – Sweet Indrajao

    • Synonyms :

  • Strī Kuṭaja

  • Vatsaka


    • Habitat :

  • Found mainly in dry and moist deciduous forests of India, it is common in southern and central India and grows well in warm tropical climates


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Small to medium-sized deciduous tree with smooth, pale-grey bark and white milky latex

  • Leaves: Simple, opposite, elliptic or lanceolate, smooth and glossy

  • Flowers: White, fragrant, with five petals, arranged in terminal clusters


    • Useful Parts :

  • Bark


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – Tikta, Kashaya

  • Guna – Laghu, Ruksha

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Sheeta


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Grahi

  • Atisaraghna

  • Krimighna


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Atisara: Bark and seed preparations are traditionally used

  • Grahani: Bark preparations are traditionally used


    • Dose

  • Bark Churna: 3–6 g

  • Seed Churna: 1–3 g

  • Kwatha: 50–100 ml


    • Formulation:

  • Indrayava Churna

  • Kutajadi Kwatha

  • Kutaja Ghana Vati
